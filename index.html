Periscope Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Periscope collects only what it needs to record, back up and play back your dashcam footage. We never sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps.

Periscope ("we", "us") makes the Periscope iPhone dashcam app. This policy covers the Periscope app and the cloud service that stores your clips. Questions go to the contact at the end.

Location is only saved inside your recordings, and only with Burn In on. While you record with Burn In on, Periscope reads your location to stamp your speed and street name onto the video. It is kept only as that text in the picture, not in your account, your settings or anywhere else on our servers. With Burn In off, Periscope does not use your location at all. To turn coordinates into a street name, your phone sends them to Apple's location service, at most every 15 seconds.

We do not collect contacts, photos from your library, advertising identifiers or analytics about how you use the app.

How we use it

We use your information only to run Periscope for you:

  • Sign you in and keep your account secure
  • Upload, store and play back your clips
  • Apply your settings, and remove your oldest trips once you pass your storage limit
  • Send you a password-reset code when you ask for one

We do not sell or rent your data, use it for advertising, share it with data brokers, or use your footage to train AI models. Only you can see your clips; we do not look at them unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

Your account keeps up to 3 hours of footage. When you go over, your oldest unlocked trips are deleted automatically, along with their cabin clips. Trips you lock are never deleted automatically, and your most recent trip is always kept.

  • On your phone: a clip stays on the device only until its upload is confirmed, then the local copy is removed.
  • One trip: tap Delete on the trip in Trips. The trip, including any cabin clips, is removed from our storage immediately.
  • Everything: Account → Delete Account. After you confirm your password, we permanently delete all your clips, your settings and your account. This cannot be undone.

Copies you have downloaded or shared yourself are outside our control and are not affected.

Periscope records only after you press record. If you leave the app while recording, it may keep recording in a small picture-in-picture window, which stays on screen the whole time. Closing that window, or stopping in the app, ends the recording.

Recording other people

Your footage may capture other drivers, pedestrians, license plates and passengers. You are responsible for recording and sharing it lawfully where you drive. With Dual Camera on, the front camera also records the inside of the car. Some places require consent to record audio or passengers, so tell them when Record Audio or Dual Camera is on.

Children

Periscope is not meant for anyone under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We protect your data with encrypted connections, hashed passwords and per-account access rules. No system is perfectly secure, so use a strong password you don't use elsewhere.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. For significant changes, we will also tell you in the app.
